# Deliverability Playbook

**How to keep your emails out of spam. Read this before sending anything.**

---

## 1. The Hard Truth About Email Deliverability

> **Templates don't prevent spam. Sender reputation does.**

Whether your email lands in Inbox or Spam is decided by:
1. **Sender reputation** — how much Gmail trusts YOUR account
2. **Authentication** — SPF, DKIM, DMARC records (handled by Gmail OAuth automatically)
3. **Sending patterns** — volume, consistency, personalization
4. **Content signals** — links, images, attachments, formatting
5. **Recipient engagement** — do people open and reply?

The "highest open-rate template" in the world won't help if Gmail thinks you're a spammer.

---

## 2. Gmail Sending Limits (Personal Accounts)

| Account Type | Daily Limit | Safe Cold-Email Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Free Gmail (@gmail.com) | 500/day | **20–50/day** |
| Google Workspace | 2,000/day | 100–200/day |
| New Gmail (< 30 days old) | 500/day | **10–20/day** |

**Why "safe" is lower than "limit":**
Gmail doesn't ban you at 500. It silently routes your emails to Spam at 50–100 if your reputation is low. You won't know until someone tells you they never got your email.

## 3. The Warmup Schedule

**Built into the app.** The cold-outreach module enforces this ramp automatically.

| Day | Max Emails/Day | What's Happening |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | 10 | Gmail observes your sending pattern |
| 4–7 | 15 | Building baseline reputation |
| 8–14 | 25 | Gradual increase |
| 15–21 | 35 | Approaching steady state |
| 22+ | 50 (cap) | Steady state if reputation is good |

**What happens if you skip warmup:**
- Days 1–3: Emails land in Inbox (Gmail is lenient with new patterns)
- Days 4–7: Some emails start going to Promotions tab
- Days 8–14: Many emails go to Spam
- Days 15+: Almost all emails in Spam

**The app auto-pauses if it detects issues** (e.g., bounce rate > 5%, or manual spam reports).

## 4. Content Rules (What Triggers Spam Filters)

### DO:
- **Personalize every email** — the app uses AI to make each body unique
- **Use plain text** — or minimal HTML; no fancy templates with images
- **Keep it short** — 100–150 words body, < 5 sentences
- **One link maximum** — your LinkedIn or portfolio, not multiple
- **Include your real signature** — name, phone, LinkedIn
- **Reference the specific role** — "Backend Developer at Acme Corp", not "a position at your company"

### DON'T:
- Use ALL CAPS in subject line or body
- Include more than 1 link
- Attach images (only attach PDF resume)
- Use URL shorteners (bit.ly etc.) — these are spam triggers
- Include "Unsubscribe" link in cold emails (looks like marketing)
- Send identical text to multiple recipients (even with name swap)
- Use phrases like "I hope this email finds you well" (spam fingerprint)
- Include emojis in subject lines
- Use "Dear Sir/Madam" (spam trigger)

### Subject Line Rules:
- Max 6 words
- No exclamation marks
- Include company name or role
- Lowercase or Title Case (not ALL CAPS)
- Examples:
  - `Application for Backend Developer — Acme Corp`
  - `Following up on the Backend role`
  - `Quick question about the SRE position`

## 5. Unique Body Generation

**The app enforces this.** Every cold email and application email has a unique body generated by AI.

How it works:
1. AI receives: JD highlights + your resume summary + recipient info
2. AI generates a 100–150 word body that is specific to THIS job at THIS company
3. The body is ALWAYS different from previous emails (even to the same company)
4. Subject lines are also AI-unique per recipient

**Why this matters:** Gmail's spam filter detects identical or near-identical bodies sent to multiple recipients. This is the #1 trigger for cold emails.

## 6. The Safety Rails (Built Into the App)

### Automatic Controls:
1. **Daily cap enforcement** — cannot be overridden in cold-outreach mode
2. **Warmup ramp** — increases daily limit automatically over 21 days
3. **Bounce detection** — if > 5% bounces, pauses sending and alerts
4. **Reply detection** — if recipient replies, stops all follow-ups for that contact
5. **Unsubscribe handling** — if someone replies "remove me", marks as unsubscribed and stops
6. **Rate limiting** — minimum 30-second gap between sends
7. **Unique content enforcement** — no two emails have identical body text

### Manual Controls:
- User can pause all sending
- User can override daily cap (with warning)
- User can force-send specific emails
- User can view sending history and reputation indicators

## 7. Authentication (OAuth vs SMTP)

| Method | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Gmail OAuth (our approach)** | Professional, threading works, no app password hack | Needs Google Cloud project, verification for production |
| **SMTP App Password** | Simpler setup | Less reliable, no threading, looks less professional |

**Gmail OAuth is better** because:
- Emails are sent via official Gmail API (Google trusts it more)
- Thread IDs work (follow-ups go in same thread)
- No need for "App Password" which Google can revoke
- OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest

**For cold outreach:** OAuth is essential. SMTP app passwords have higher spam rates because Google treats them as "less secure apps."

## 8. Legal Compliance

### India: Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP) 2023
- Cold emails to individuals require **consent** or fall under **legitimate interest** exception
- For job-seeking: contacting HR at their professional email is generally considered legitimate interest
- Must include a way to opt out (reply "remove")
- Must not send to individuals who have opted out

### Global: CAN-SPAM (US) / GDPR (EU) / PECR (UK)
- Cold B2B emails are generally legal (not B2C)
- Must include: sender identity, physical address (or company address), opt-out mechanism
- Opt-out must be honored within 10 days
- No deceptive subject lines

### Best Practices (Covered by the App):
1. **Real sender identity** — sent from YOUR Gmail with YOUR name
2. **Professional purpose** — applying for jobs or contacting HR about roles
3. **Opt-out available** — "Reply REMOVE to opt out" (included in templates)
4. **No deception** — subject lines reflect actual content
5. **Respect opt-outs** — automatic if someone replies "remove"

## 9. Monitoring Deliverability

### Signs Your Emails Are Going to Spam:
1. Zero replies to cold outreach (normal reply rate: 5–15%)
2. People say they "never got your email"
3. Bounce rate increases
4. Your emails appear in "Sent" but not in recipient's visible inbox

### What To Do:
1. **Check directly** — send a test email to another account you control
2. **Use Gmail's "Sent" folder** — open a sent email and check the delivery status
3. **Reduce volume** — if issues appear, lower daily cap by 50%
4. **Increase personalization** — more unique = better deliverability
5. **Check blacklists** — use mxtoolbox.com to check if your domain/IP is blacklisted

### The App Monitors:
- Bounce rate (auto-pause at > 5%)
- Send success rate
- Daily volume vs safe limits
- Time between sends (minimum 30s gap)
